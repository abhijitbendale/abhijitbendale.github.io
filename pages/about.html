About Me

I'm a researcher, engineer, and applied AI leader with 15+ years of experience building and scaling production-grade AI systems across computer vision, LLMs, and multimodal AI.

I was Founder & CTO of Two Platforms, where I raised a $20M seed round from Jio Platforms and Naver. At Two Platforms, I architected SUTRA, a multilingual LLM platform, and led enterprise AI products generating $10M+ ARR. One of our largest deployments was multimodal AI search for Jio AI Cloud, serving 400M+ customers.

Our work spanned multilingual LLMs, voice, video, and consumer AI, including ChatSUTRA, SUTRA's multilingual tokenizer, SUTRA HiFi, and SUTRA Avatar. We also built consumer AI products including ZAPPY and AutoCameo.

Before that, I was CTO of Samsung's STAR Labs, where I led the development of NEON, a real-time digital human platform launched at CES 2020 and subsequently deployed in the APAC market. STAR Labs was later acquired internally by Samsung Electronics.

From 2015 to 2019, I was at Samsung Research America, where I helped architect Samsung's on-device AI platform, commercialized as Bixby Vision, powering AI experiences across 100M+ Galaxy devices worldwide.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science, specializing in Computer Vision and Machine Learning, from the University of Colorado, where I was advised by Prof. Terrance Boult. I have also conducted research at MIT with Prof. James DiCarlo and Prof. Ramesh Raskar, as well as at EPFL in Switzerland and SRI International. My work has resulted in numerous patents, and my research publications have been cited 4,000+ times.
