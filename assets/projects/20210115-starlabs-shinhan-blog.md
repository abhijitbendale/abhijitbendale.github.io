## Samsung NEON and Shinhan Bank: Bringing Next-Generation AI to Banking

At Samsung STAR Labs, we developed **NEON** to bring a human presence to digital interactions. Through our partnership with **Shinhan Bank**, we are bringing a next-generation AI experience to banking, combining photorealistic Artificial Humans, real-time conversation, and an immersive customer interface.

### Bringing NEON into Banking

The partnership brings together NEON's Artificial Human platform and Shinhan Bank's banking expertise to deliver a more natural customer experience. Virtual banking representatives provide a conversational interface for customer assistance, helping make banking services more accessible and approachable.

### NEON Frame: Built for Face-to-Face Interaction

**[NEON Frame](https://www.neon.life/frame/)** brings this experience into physical spaces with a life-size Artificial Human. The product supports **4K resolution, 700-nit brightness, and a 178-degree viewing angle**, with 55-, 65-, and 75-inch display configurations. A high-fidelity multi-speaker array supports clear, natural conversations.

Frame is cloud connected, with customization software for onboarding a business-specific NEON WorkForce. Its accessory options include a vision sensor, card reader, fingerprint sensor, and touchless payments, providing ways to connect conversational interaction with banking workflows. These are capabilities of the Frame product family, with the configuration tailored to each deployment.

### A Human Interface to Enterprise AI

The NEON platform combines real-time rendering, speech, facial expressions, gaze, and gestures to support natural interaction. In banking, this creates a human-facing interface that connects customers with domain-specific services and knowledge.

The Shinhan Bank partnership brings **NEON WorkForce** into banking, applying our Artificial Human technology to a customer-facing enterprise product. It advances our goal of making AI services intuitive to use through conversation and human presence.
