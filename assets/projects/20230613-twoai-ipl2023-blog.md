## Two Platforms powers Jio VIP Box at IPL 2023

Published: [From June 13, 2023](https://www.two.ai/blog/key-highlights-from-ipl-2023)

![](blog-images/ipl2023-title.avif)

During IPL 2023, AutoCameo was used as part of Jio's VIP Box experience to deliver personalized video messages to cricket fans. The launch handled nearly 300,000 fan requests, generated more than 2 million views, and saw significant sharing and engagement across social media.

The campaign marked AutoCameo's debut within the Jio VIP Box fan experience, where fans could request personalized messages from IPL players.

### Key highlights from AutoCameo during IPL 2023

- Nearly **300,000 requests** were submitted by fans for personalized messages from their favorite IPL players.
- At peak load, the **AutoCameo API produced about 160 personalized videos per minute**, without outages and with limited pressure on backend infrastructure.
- In roughly **12 days**, AutoCameo videos accumulated **more than 2 million views** and broad engagement across social media.

![](blog-images/ipl2023-engagement.webp)

Birthday and anniversary greetings featuring players from teams such as **Royal Challengers Bangalore** and **Chennai Super Kings** were among the most frequently requested messages. Fans also shared their personalized AutoCameo videos with friends and communities on social platforms.

The IPL 2023 campaign demonstrated how personalized generative-video experiences could be delivered at large scale. The team envisioned AutoCameo expanding beyond cricket and sports into additional industries and markets worldwide.
