Title: Samsung STAR Labs and CJ sign MoU
Date: Oct 29, 2020
#Group: Samsung (STAR) Labs
Title Image: blog-images/samsung-cj-mou-title.jpg
Representative Text: STAR Labs signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with CJ OliveNetworks on the Oct 23, 2020 to collaborate on the artificial human business. CJ plans to proactively respond to the rapidly evolving media content industry by leveraging NEON; as a first step, the company intends to collaborate with a virtual influencer to create new forms of content.
