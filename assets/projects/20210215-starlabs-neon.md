Title: NEON: Building Artificial Humans at Samsung STAR Labs
Date: Feb 15, 2021
#Group: Samsung (STAR) Labs

Representative Text: NEON was an Artificial Human platform developed at Samsung STAR Labs to explore a more natural interface between people and intelligent machines. At its core was CORE R3 — Reality, Realtime, Responsive, which focused on generating photorealistic digital humans and their behavior in real time, while SPECTRA was envisioned as the intelligence layer for learning, memory, emotion, and personalization
